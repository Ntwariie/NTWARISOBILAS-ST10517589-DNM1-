About Paws & Hope Animal Rescue

Paws & Hope Animal Rescue is a hypothetical organisation developed for this assessment. Its purpose is to promote responsible animal care and make it easier for community members to learn about rescue and support opportunities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support animals in need and encourage responsible care, adoption, fostering and community involvement.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a caring community where animals are treated responsibly and have opportunities for safe and suitable homes.

Our Values

  • Compassion towards animals
  • Responsible pet ownership
  • Community involvement
  • Respect and kindness
